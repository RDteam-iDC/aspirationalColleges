How the filters and tiers work

Award matches the filter_value column, and falls back to program_award when no row carries that value. Subject matches program.awarded_in_parent first, then program.awarded_in. Each award and subject pair shows as its own grid.

Tier follows the reco sheet. For each course and cluster, colleges are ordered by their best rank and numbered 1, 2, 3 and so on. The first 30 are split 10 / 10 / 10 into Tier 1, 2 and 3. With fewer than 30 colleges the split is xxy, xyy or yyy with x larger than y (29 gives 10/10/9, 7 gives 3/2/2, 5 gives 2/2/1, 3 gives 1/1/1). Rank values themselves are not shown. With no cluster chosen, every cluster that has ranks for the course gets its own block.
