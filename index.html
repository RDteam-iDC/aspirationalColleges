How the filters and tiers work

Award matches the filter_value column, and falls back to program_award when no row carries that value. Subject matches program.awarded_in_parent first, then program.awarded_in. Each award and subject pair shows as its own grid.

Tier follows the reco sheet and appears as a column in each grid. Within each course and cluster, colleges are ordered by their best rank and the first 30 are split 10 / 10 / 10 into Tier 1, 2 and 3. With fewer than 30 colleges the split is xxy, xyy or yyy with x larger than y (29 gives 10/10/9, 7 gives 3/2/2, 5 gives 2/2/1, 3 gives 1/1/1). With a cluster chosen the grid shows that cluster's Tier and rank; with none chosen it shows a Tier and rank pair for every cluster that has ranks for the course.
